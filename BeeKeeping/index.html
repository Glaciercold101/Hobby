01 Is Beekeeping For You?

Your own words: gentle, observant, neat temperament.

✅ You need

Reasonable greenery, local association (advice + insurance), taster day, bee inspectors (free), magazines: Beekeepers Quarterly, Bee Culture, Bee Craft, American Bee Journal.

⏳ Time & Sting

Not time consuming but weekly late spring–early summer. Bees work on their own time. Sting: swelling, itchy 2 days.

02 Fascinating Facts

  • Essential to life. 1/3 of what we eat pollinated.
  • Use sun as compass, see UV light.
  • Only insect producing human food. Defecates outside in mid-air.
  • Wings 11,400/min = buzz. 12-15 mph, 6 miles per trip.
  • 90,000 miles = 3x globe = 1lb honey. 50-100 flowers per trip.
  • Average bee 1/12 tsp lifetime. 556 workers per pound.
  • 17-20 lbs honey → 1 lb wax.
  • Bumblebees solve travelling salesman. Honeycomb 120° perfect hexagon, least wax.
  • Honey airtight never spoils — King Tut's tomb edible.
  • Bee brain sesame seed but calculates distance & efficiency.

Compound Eye

5 eyes: 2 compound + 3 ocelli. Sees movement instantly, human movement = aggression.

Pollen Baskets

Hind leg corbicula, colour = flower source. Brushed from body to basket.

03 Pollination & Importance

Bees stay constant to one flower type while it yields — correct pollination, better quality, higher yield, earlier harvest. $8-10B US yearly. Einstein 4-year quote is myth — you flagged doubtful, correct.

04 From Skep to Modern Hive

Early: woven grass baskets, clay, bark, hollow logs — bees killed to harvest. 1852 Reverend Langstroth discovered bee space, invented movable frame & modern hive. Now observe without killing.

05 Inside the Colony

Annual Cycle

Expands spring for nectar flow, contracts into winter cluster. Queen leaves with half workers to make new colony.

Nesting

Vertical wax combs, chewed with mandibles. Nursery ball 35°C, ring pollen, honey shell insulation. Healthy larvae glistening white.

06 Meet the Bees

👑 Queen — 5 years

Prime 2 years. Longer abdomen, wings half down. Dark protected centre. Leaves 1-2x year to mate/swarm. Stores sperm in spermatheca, lays 1000/day every 10 sec, 1.5M lifetime. Pheromones control colony.

👷 Worker — 30d summer / 100d+ winter

Female, cannot mate (except laying workers). Age-related jobs: Day1 orienting → 2-6 cleaning → 4-12 feeding brood → 6-18 building → 8-18 storing → 18-21 guarding → 20-end foraging. Glands produce milk, wax, scent.

🚁 Drone — 500-1000/hive

Male passengers, larger body stronger wings, cannot sting, fed by workers, roams hives, only job mate queen then dies, DCA congregation.

07 Anatomy & Superpowers

Head, thorax, abdomen, 6 legs, 4 wings with tiny teeth locking, 2 antennae, 5 eyes, proboscis straw tongue, mandibles, 2 stomachs (eating + nectar). 4 stages Egg→Larvae→Pupae→Adult.

Capacity

One hive 60lb (27kg)+ surplus good season, average 25lb. One frame 2.25kg. Can fill 2/3 boxes/week if nectar. Needs 1L water. Strong colony 2-3x need — surplus is yours.

Sting

Only females sting, tears from body → dies. Queen sting repeats vs rival queen only.

08 How Bees Talk

Food sharing: trophallaxis passes pheromones, learns demand. Pheromones 17-39 types: queen safe, alarm, brood recognition, drone, egg marking, footprint, forager.

Dance: 3 types — swarm new nest, encourage new queen, food. Figure-eight on comb, vigour = richness, rate/duration = distance. Even in dark hot crowded hive.

09 What Bees Make

Honey

18% water 35% glucose 40% fructose 4% sugars 3% other 181 minor components. White mild expensive, heather best jelly slightly bitter, dark strong darkens. Storage indefinite if <20% water 80% sealed air-tight <10°C. Granulation natural. Fix: soft set warm stir 10% seed, or 42°C 24-48h seed oilseed rape.

Wax, Propolis, Pollen, Nectar

Wax: glands under abdomen, white→dark, 10lb honey→1lb wax. Uses candles, cosmetics, pharma, batik, polish.
Propolis: resin buds wounds + saliva <6mm gaps sealant dark brown antibiotic.
Pollen: protein powder to baskets to brood cells honey preservative.
Nectar: sweet to attract, concentrated + enzymes → honey.

10 Your First Hive

Ideal Spec

2 cu ft stick ONE size. England Modified National Langstroth. Bee space bee-tight only entrance. Wood 19-22mm pine treated. Wax foundation room temp board flat don't overstock brittle.

Types

WBC double wall attractive small, Single Commercial Langstroth Dadant bigger National Smith smaller, Rose vertical same size no excluder higher EFB risk, Top Bar horizontal not preference.

Components A-F

A Floor on stand not floor damp creatures Varroa mesh. B Brood 11 frames. C Excluder keeps queen/drones brood wire Waldron gentler. D Supers shorter 3-4 needed above brood. E Crownboard clear bees before harvest. F Roof watertight mesh ventilation.

11 Handling & Equipment

Suit all-in-one hood, gloves or rubber washing up, Wellington boots. Never open late night. Mobile if alone. Smoker always lighted masks alarm pheromones doesn't hurt. Fuel coconut husk pinecones cardboard egg boxes wood shavings sack not smelly. Hive Tool Standard J-type brush.
